Professional biography

Dr. Zohair Mohamed Kamel is an Associate Professor in the Department of Business Administration, College of Humanities and Administrative Studies, at Onaizah Colleges. His specialisation is human resources.

He holds a Ph.D. in Business Administration from Al-Neelain University (2003), a master’s degree in Administrative Sciences from the University of Gezira (1997) and a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from the University of Baghdad (1989). He has held academic and administrative positions in Yemen and Saudi Arabia, and has taught courses in human resources, organisational behaviour, strategic management, project management and entrepreneurship.

He has published research in academic journals, authored a book on the fundamentals of purchasing and warehousing, and delivered training programmes for companies, associations and government bodies. He has also served on quality, accreditation and community service committees.

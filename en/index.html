01 Academic introduction

Research and training in service of the community

His academic work centres on human resource management, with an interest in research that serves the community.

He is an Associate Professor in the Department of Business Administration, College of Humanities and Administrative Studies, at Onaizah Colleges. His courses include human resource management, organisational behaviour, strategic management and entrepreneurship.

His research covers topics such as work stress, performance evaluation, organisational loyalty and green innovation, and he delivers training programmes for companies, associations and government bodies.
